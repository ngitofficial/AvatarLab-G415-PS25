# AvatarLab-G415-PS25

AvatarLab is an AI-driven platform that produces realistic talking head videos by merging advanced voice replication with dynamic facial animation. It empowers users to build believable AI personas that can deliver speech with precise lip-syncing and natural expressions, based on audio or text inputs.

**Project Uses**

-Virtual characters for use in gaming, social networking, and virtual worlds
-AI-powered dubbing solutions for film and video content
-Communication support tools for individuals with speech challenges
-Digital hosts for educational, promotional, or news-related material
-More lively and interactive online meetings using animated avatars
